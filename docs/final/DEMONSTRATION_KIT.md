# Visitor and working-demonstration kit

Event: 23 October 2026, 9:30 AM-12:00 PM; report 9:15 AM, LA-LHC Complex Foyer, IIT Bombay. A visitor should be able to operate the learner path without an explanation from the team.

## Three-minute independent try-out

Open **https://promptshala.vercel.app/try** or scan the poster QR. No sign-in or AI key is required. It uses fictional science material, local CRAFT checklist feedback and source scaffolds. It does not save course progress or send material to an AI provider.

1. **Write a clear request (about 45 seconds).** Click **Check my request** on "Explain evaporation." Notice the missing context. Click **Try a clearer example**, then check again. Identify one added classroom detail. The checklist score describes the request, not correctness of the eventual answer.
2. **Prepare a teaching draft (about 45 seconds).** Choose **Next: prepare a draft**. Read the supplied passage, choose Worksheet, and click **Prepare my draft**. Edit one instruction to fit your learners. A numbered source label is a prompt to check evidence; it is not proof.
3. **Review before use (about 90 seconds).** Choose **Next: review before use**. Enter the audience and learning objective if needed. Rate each criterion and give a specific supporting observation. Essential checks for evidence and privacy can flag a draft for revision. Export a review if all required entries are complete. Click **Start over** before the next visitor.

Do not enter pupil names, account passwords, private materials or API keys into the public demonstration. Refreshing or starting over clears its temporary work. Downloaded exports remain on the visitor's device.

## Longer learner demonstration

Use a dedicated synthetic **participant** account created for the event, with a non-personal name and no real teaching records. Keep its credentials on an attended team card or a controlled device; never print administrator credentials on the poster. Visitors may also choose the public path or create their own account under the approved signup policy.

Show the plain-language homepage; optional AI-use onboarding; a short lesson and chapter check; retryable quiz; CRAFT revision; helper specification and versioned tests; source claim review; saved progress and completion checklist. Demonstrate a certificate only from genuinely eligible saved work. Do not fill passing scores directly to make the demo look complete.

Use course AI only after confirming a successful configured call, safe-use acknowledgement and permitted fictional content. If unavailable, explain the visible fallback/manual path. Never describe a prepared excerpt as a fresh provider response.
