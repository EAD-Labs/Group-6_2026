# Learning Resource Curation and Copyright Plan

## Content policy

PromptShala will use team-authored explanations, appropriately attributed open material, and links or embeds from original publishers. The team will not download, copy, or rehost copyrighted videos. Every external item must have a recorded source, owner, access method, and reuse basis before it enters the product.

## Attribution format

For adapted open material, display an attribution near the lesson footer and record it in the content register.

> Adapted from “[Title]” by [Creator/Organisation], licensed under [licence with link]. Changes: simplified and reorganised for beginner school teachers.

Use the [Creative Commons BY 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [Creative Commons attribution guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/) when checking credit requirements. For UNESCO adaptations, use the licence stated by UNESCO and retain the ShareAlike requirement.

## Content register fields

Every external resource must record:

- resource title and publisher;
- canonical URL;
- module and lesson placement;
- licence or permission basis;
- link, embed, quotation, or adaptation method;
- attribution text;
- accessibility status, including captions or transcript alternative;
- date last verified;
- reviewer and approval status.

## Review checklist before publication

- The URL resolves to the original publisher.
- The resource directly supports a stated learning outcome.
- Licence or embedding permission has been checked and recorded.
- No video or copyrighted file has been downloaded or rehosted.
- Any excerpt is short, necessary, and attributed.
- Captions or an equivalent text alternative are available.
- The language and examples are suitable for beginner school teachers.
- The material does not request or expose student personal data.
- Facts and product instructions remain current.
- A team member has reviewed the resource and the client has approved required curriculum decisions.

## Prototype placeholders

Until final client approval, use labelled placeholders rather than unverified third-party content:

- `Optional video: Prompt engineering with Gemini (embedded from the official Google for Education YouTube channel)`
- `External reference: Open the official NotebookLM guide in a new tab`
- `Source note: This explanation is team-authored and informed by the references listed in the lesson footer`

The prototype must not imply that Google, UNESCO, or another source endorses PromptShala.
