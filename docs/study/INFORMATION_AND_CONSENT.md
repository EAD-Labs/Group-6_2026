# Participant information and study consent

**Review-ready template. The coordinator must fill the contact, storage and dates below before using it with participants.** Study: PromptShala beginner-teacher usability and learning-task pilot; planned 16 October-6 November 2026. Client/coordinator organisation: Centre for Educational Technology, IIT Bombay.

## Information for participants

We are testing how clearly PromptShala helps teachers practise writing requests for AI, checking sources and reviewing teaching drafts. We want to learn where people need help and what should be improved. We are not assessing your employment or claiming the software has already improved teaching.

You may take part in a first session of about 75 minutes, optional self-paced course use and an agreed exit session. You will use fictional classroom examples. You may think aloud, revise a request, check a draft against supplied notes, and answer a short interview. Tell us about any access needs; we can adjust the activity, pause or stop.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop without giving a reason. This does not remove your normal access to the course or any entitlement. A certificate depends on the course's recorded completion requirements, not agreement to participate in this study. Study consent is separate from the course's safe-use acknowledgement.

We will record a pseudonymous ID, teaching context needed for interpretation, device/browser, task completion, time, help required, errors/recovery, rubric ratings and optional interview notes. We do not need pupil names, real pupil work, account passwords, API keys or private school documents. Please do not enter those. Do not share your password with the observer.

Screen or audio recording is optional and requires a separate choice below. We can take structured notes without recording. A quotation is used only with permission and after removing identifying details. The public final report contains summaries and de-identified examples, not names or raw account exports. The small sample may still be recognisable from distinctive details; we will remove those details where possible.

The course and a live AI provider have their own processing notice. The public visitor exercise uses local methods and no provider call; authenticated live AI should only use permitted, non-sensitive fictional material. A human checklist/rubric score is not proof of a factual answer or teaching quality.
