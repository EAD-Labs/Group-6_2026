# Classroom colour

The composition should be recognised from across a busy exhibition. A deep teal headline area announces the audience and purpose immediately, while a short classroom example turns an abstract technology into a familiar teaching task. Precise alignment keeps this bold first impression clear and professional.

Warm cream, mint, coral and golden yellow give the page energy without turning each section into a different theme. Colour groups related ideas: a mint learner panel, a teal development process, warm feedback notes and a golden invitation to try the app. The craftsmanship lies in consistent contrasts, balanced colour areas and carefully proportioned spacing.

Typography establishes the reading order before a visitor reads a sentence. A large sans serif identifies teachers; a warm serif phrase names classroom use. Short, bold headings carry the story, while concise paragraphs remain legible at A0. Refined line breaks and optical spacing make the denser composition feel intentional rather than crowded.

Real application panels anchor the teaching journey. Their browser-rendered text remains vector artwork, so visitors can inspect the detail without the pixelation of a flattened screenshot. Connectors show progression, restrained step markers guide attention and a compact review panel explains the teacher's final judgement.

The final pass removes ambiguity rather than adding ornament. Every arrow should connect a meaningful step, every colour should clarify a group and every line should earn its space. Full-page and high-zoom inspection check that the composition is meticulously finished from the opening headline to the closing QR code.

## Production notes

- A0 portrait, 841 × 1189 mm, with generous internal margins.
- Deep teal `#123B43`, teal `#176F70`, cream `#FCF7ED`, mint `#C9E6D8`, coral `#F28168`, golden yellow `#F4CB62`.
- Instrument Sans Regular/Bold and Instrument Serif Regular, bundled under their OFL licences.
- Keep the six course topics present through natural headings and concise copy.
- Vector app panels show the real public application with fictional classroom material. Only soft interface shadows remain raster.
- Describe implemented features and planned teacher trials separately.
- No course-rubric terminology is needed as a decorative label.
- Design guidance: Canvas Design and Theme Factory from `anthropics/skills`, with typography and restraint informed by the existing Apple Design skill.
