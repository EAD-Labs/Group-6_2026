Purpose: Class 6 science; explain evaporation using observation. No pupil data.

SOURCE
Water in an open dish can slowly change into water vapour. This change is called evaporation. The speed depends on conditions, including temperature and exposed surface area.

In a classroom observation, compare equal amounts of water in identical dishes. Record the conditions and the change over time. A single observation has limits; repeat and compare before drawing a general conclusion.
